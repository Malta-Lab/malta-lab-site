The survey **Fairness in Deep Learning: A Survey on Vision and Language Research** was published in *ACM Computing Surveys* (vol. 57, no. 6).

It reviews research on fairness in deep learning models for computer vision and natural language processing.

**Authors:** Parraga, O.; Móre, M. D.; Oliveira, C. M.; Gavenski, N. S.; Kupssinskü, L. S.; Medronha, A.; Moura, L. V.; Simões, G. S.; Barros, R. C.
