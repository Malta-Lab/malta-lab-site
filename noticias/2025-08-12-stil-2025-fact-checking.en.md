The paper **Automated Fact-Checking in Brazilian Portuguese: Resources and Baselines** was accepted at the **16th Brazilian Symposium in Information and Human Language Technology (STIL 2025)**, co-located with BRACIS 2025 in Fortaleza, Brazil.

The work brings together resources and baselines for automated fact-checking in Brazilian Portuguese.

**Authors:** Delucis, M. M.; Fraga, L.; Parraga, O.; Mattjie, C.; Ravazio, R.; Barros, R. C.; Kupssinskü, L. S.

[Read the paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.5753/stil.2025.37820)
