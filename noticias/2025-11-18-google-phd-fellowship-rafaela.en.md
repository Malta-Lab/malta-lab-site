MALTA PhD student **Rafaela Ravazio** was selected for the **Google PhD Fellowship Program** in Health Research. She continues her thesis under Professor Rodrigo Barros, now with funding and mentoring from Google.

## The program

Google's program supports doctoral research in Computer Science and related fields. This edition awarded more than US$10 million to 255 PhD students from 35 countries, across twelve areas. It was the first time students from Latin America and the Middle East were included.

- Funding for up to two years; in South America, US$15,000 a year for research costs, including travel.
- Guidance from a Google mentor throughout the program.

## The research

Rafaela aims to combine genomic (DNA), transcriptomic (RNA), proteomic, and metabolomic data into a single representation, using AI to find patterns in **Parkinson's disease** and to separate groups of patients. The hypothesis is that Parkinson's is made up of different sub-diseases, which would help explain why patients with the same diagnosis respond differently to treatment. MALTA has funding from the Michael J. Fox Foundation for this line of research.

Rafaela holds a degree in Physics, with a focus on Medical Physics, from PUCRS. For her master's in Biomedical Gerontology she worked with CT scans to predict the need for intubation in COVID-19 cases, her first contact with machine learning. When the fellowship was announced, she was on a visiting PhD period in London, planned to run until March 2026.
