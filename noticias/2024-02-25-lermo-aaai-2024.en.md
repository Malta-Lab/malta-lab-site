**LERMO**, an AI-powered web game for Brazilian Sign Language (LIBRAS) recognition, was presented at the **AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI 2024)**, in the EAAI-24 *Mentored Undergraduate Research Challenge*.

**Paper:** LERMO: A Novel Web Game for AI-Enhanced Sign Language Recognition

**Authors:** Medronha, A.; Lima, L.; Claudio, J.; Kupssinskü, L. S.; Barros, R. C.
