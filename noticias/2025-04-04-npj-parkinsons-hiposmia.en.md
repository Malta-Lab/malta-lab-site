The paper **Hyposmia in Parkinson's Disease: Exploring Selective Odour Loss** was published in *npj Parkinson's Disease* on April 4, 2025.

The study looks at selective odour loss as an early marker of Parkinson's disease and was carried out in international collaboration.

**Authors:** Mitchell, E.; Mattjie, C.; Bestwick, J.; Barros, R. C.; Schuh, A.; Simonet, C.; Noyce, A.

[Read the paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-025-00922-3)
